# Social avatar and close-friends apps: Bondee, Zepeto, Locket (and BeReal as a contrast)

Scope: how avatar rooms and close-friend sharing drive daily opens and virality, why some of these apps faded, and how they make money. These are inputs for Lifey (an isometric 大頭妹-style room, a chibi doll, daily entries, family/partner sharing; ages 13–35, mostly female, Taiwan first). Research date: 2026-10-20. All metrics are company claims or third-party estimates unless noted.

## Bondee: why it went viral in Taiwan and Southeast Asia, its room and status features, retention after the spike, the privacy controversy, and its current status

### Takeaway
Bondee (Metadream, Singapore) launched on 17 Jan 2023. During Lunar New Year it went to #1 in Taiwan and Southeast Asia, helped by heavy ads and invite-link sharing. Its features were cute 3D avatars, decoratable rooms, friends visiting each other's rooms and leaving notes, and a 50-friend "close friends only" cap. It collapsed within about two months. Downloads fell from 2.6M (Feb) to 0.4M (Mar 2023). Three causes are documented: credit-card leak rumours (denied by the company), the revelation that it was a relaunch of China's Jelly/啫喱 and that its terms listed "中國台灣", and novelty fade made worse by the small friend cap. According to Wikipedia, it was pulled from the stores in March 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **Launch and setup:** Launched 17 Jan 2023 and announced 15 Nov 2022. Metadream acquired the IP of True.ly (the developer of Jelly) in May 2022 — [Wikipedia: Bondee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bondee)
- **Features:** Users customise a 3D avatar (hair, clothes, shoes, accessories) and furnish a virtual room. The avatar is "figure-style" and is used to show your mood or situation to friends. Activities include camping, swinging, dancing and sailing, plus visiting friends' rooms and leaving notes. Reviewers compared it to Animal Crossing, Habbo Hotel and The Sims — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bondee)
- **Close-friends cap:** "Close friends only", with each circle capped at 50 friends. Friends can chat and leave notes on walls; outings include picnics, camping and sailing. The in-app currency is "B-Beans", with a planned blockchain wallet and NFTs. Bondee was #1 in Social Networking on the App Store in Singapore, Malaysia, the Philippines and Thailand, and top 10 in Japan (as of 3 Feb 2023). Local brands and public figures were already building spaces — [Mindshare POV, 3 Feb 2023](https://www.mindshareworld.com/news/pov-what-is-bondee/)
- **Downloads:** 2 million App Store downloads within two weeks of launch. In the last week of January 2023 it topped weekly downloads in Singapore, the Philippines, Thailand, Malaysia and Taiwan (data.ai) — [Epoch Times summary](https://www.theepochtimes.com/popular-metaverse-app-bondee-fades-fast-as-ccp-ties-are-revealed_5248765.html). This is a secondary source; it is consistent with the [Vulcan Post](https://vulcanpost.com/825490/bondee-viral-social-media-app-fail-reasons/) figure of 2M in two weeks.
- **Taiwan rankings:** From 24 Jan 2023 it was #1 in App Store Social for several days running. Its launch regions included Singapore, Thailand, Japan, Korea, Malaysia, the Philippines and Taiwan, which the app labelled "中國台灣" — [風傳媒 Storm Media](https://www.storm.mg/article/4718899)
- **Why it spread in Taiwan:** Heavy advertising during Lunar New Year, plus users and influencers sharing invite links. The INSIDE article (24 Jan 2023) flagged that some screens still showed 啫喱 branding, that the privacy policy listed Taiwan as "中國台灣" with an exception clause for Chinese users, and that data might be held in China. Jelly had been pulled from the Chinese App Store in Feb 2022 after scam-call and security complaints — [INSIDE](https://www.inside.com.tw/article/30523-what-is-bondee); [經理人 Manager Today](https://www.managertoday.com.tw/articles/view/66347)
- **Dcard reception:** Taiwanese Dcard users described the core appeal as the 核心摯友 (core close friends) feature, limited to 50 friends — [Dcard](https://www.dcard.tw/f/talk/p/241063134)
- **Downloads collapse:** iOS and Google Play downloads fell from 2.6 million (Feb 2023) to 400,000 (Mar 2023) (data.ai). "Many users didn't just abandon Bondee, they also started sharing social media posts on how to delete accounts." "Some users said they simply got bored with it." — [Bloomberg Businessweek via The Straits Times, 28 Apr 2023](https://cis.smu.edu.sg/sites/cis.smu.edu.sg/files/news/28%20Apr%202023%20-%20The%20Straits%20Times%20Online.pdf)
- **The 50-friend cap as a weakness:** Prof. Lim Sun Sun (SMU) said the cap "was both its value proposition and its Achilles' heel… There were simply too few people to generate traction." — [Straits Times / Bloomberg](https://cis.smu.edu.sg/sites/cis.smu.edu.sg/files/news/28%20Apr%202023%20-%20The%20Straits%20Times%20Online.pdf)
- **China ties and trust:** Most Metadream employees were in China. It shared legal and finance staff with Jelly's Chinese owner. It was led by Yang Yuxiang, a financier with two decades at state-backed firms. On 15 Feb 2023 Metadream acknowledged that Bondee was based on Jelly. Korean users criticised it for collecting IMEI numbers. Kim Sangkyun (Kyung Hee University) said: "You can create the most aesthetically pleasing metaverse platform, but if people feel misled or unsafe, they aren't going to stick around… Trust is everything." — [Straits Times / Bloomberg](https://cis.smu.edu.sg/sites/cis.smu.edu.sg/files/news/28%20Apr%202023%20-%20The%20Straits%20Times%20Online.pdf); [Bloomberg original (paywalled)](https://brp-prod-bcc.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-04-26/bondee-app-downloads-plummet-as-china-ties-come-to-light)
- **Other failure factors:** New registrations were suspended for a time. Users reported spam calls and messages. Users were sceptical of the NFT plans, which were never launched. Vulcan Post's verdict: "the app lacked enough essence that would make users want to stay"; the features were not unique, and WhatsApp already covers close-friend chat — [Vulcan Post, 15 May 2023](https://vulcanpost.com/825490/bondee-viral-social-media-app-fail-reasons/)
- **Credit-card allegations:** Users alleged unauthorised card charges. Metadream denied this and said the app does not collect financial information — [NME](https://www.nme.com/news/bondee-app-credit-card-scam-allegations-metadream-denies-in-statement-3388459); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bondee)
- **Shutdown:** The app was removed from the stores on 4 Mar 2026, with its main features disabled. By 31 Mar 2026 Metadream had shut it down on both Google Play and the App Store — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bondee). Only Wikipedia reports this, and the page is tagged as promotional.
- **Brand-safety debate:** Campaign Asia covered whether Bondee was a brand-safety red flag — [Campaign Asia](https://www.campaignasia.com/article/is-bondee-a-brand-safety-red-flag/483501)

### Inferences
- The core loop was visual presence plus a small circle: your avatar shows your mood or status, and friends drop by your room and leave notes. That loop was strong enough to reach #1 across Asia in a week, which confirms demand among young Asian users for a "cute room + close friends" format. It did not create a habit beyond novelty, because there was little to *do* each day besides decorating and visiting.
- The 50-friend cap helps intimacy but means that for each new user the network is empty or thin. For Lifey this suggests the room and journal must be worth opening **solo**, with partner/family sharing as an extra rather than the reason to open the app.
- Trust and data provenance are existential in Taiwan. The "中國台灣" wording and the hidden China origin were decisive in Taiwan. Lifey stores intimate diary data, so it should be explicit about where data lives (Cloudflare), who can see what, and that it has no China ties.
- A cosmetics currency (B-Beans) combined with NFT plans created suspicion. Plain cosmetics or subscription monetization is safer.

### Gaps
- No Taiwan-only download or DAU numbers for Bondee were found; Taiwan appears only in the data.ai weekly #1 list and the Storm Media ranking.
- No Bondee retention cohorts (D1/D30), DAU, or revenue were found.
- The 2026 shutdown is reported only by Wikipedia and has no official Metadream source; it needs verification.

## Zepeto: users (age, gender, Asia share), the cosmetics economy, creator items, and revenue

### Takeaway
Zepeto (Naver Z, Korea) is the long-lived avatar/fashion counterpart to Bondee. It has over 400M cumulative registrations, but only about 20M MAU (Jan 2022). Its core users are female teens and early-20s, and most are outside Korea. It monetizes through avatar items and a creator marketplace (2B items sold by early 2022). The top creator earned $500K gross in 2021. Despite its scale it loses money heavily: 2025 revenue was ₩66.3B with an operating loss of ₩49.4B, and the company has negative equity.

### Cited Findings
- **Users and items (Jan 2022):** 290M registered users and 20M MAU (up from 10M MAU in May 2020). The core audience is "female teenagers and early twenty-somethings", concentrated in South Korea and China. 2 billion items had been sold since the 2018 launch (600M by mid-2020). The top creator earned $500,000 gross in 2021. Brand collaborations include Gucci, Ralph Lauren, Blackpink and Selena Gomez. Naver Z launched a $100M creator fund, after a 2021 $190M Series B led by SoftBank Vision Fund II — [TechCrunch, 27 Jan 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/01/27/south-koreas-naver-z-launches-100m-fund-for-metaverse-creators)
- **Gender and age:** Reported as about 70% female, mostly aged 13–21 — [TYN Magazine](https://tynmagazine.com/asias-largest-metaverse-platform-zepeto-ramps-up-global-expansion/). A Korean report (2021) said teenagers were 80% of users and non-Koreans about 90% — [KED Global, Oct 2021](https://www.kedglobal.com/newsView/ked202110130012)
- **Later registrations:** More than 400M registered users (2023) — [LA Magazine contributor content](https://lamag.com/contributor-content/a-new-social-experience-the-creative-power-of-naver-zs-avatar-platform/). This is sponsored content.
- **Revenue 2025:** Naver Z's consolidated revenue was ₩66.3B, down 14.4% year on year. The operating loss was ₩49.4B, 18.6% narrower after cutting payment fees and labour costs. Total equity was −₩315.3B, with capital impairment ₩77.4B larger than the year before. Zepeto Live streamers rose 126% in 2025. Live revenue comes from tips, item purchases and fan subscriptions. AI streamer "Nami" debuted in Jan 2026 to about 3,000 viewers in Korean and about 8,000 in English — [Newsis, 10–11 Apr 2026](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20260410_0003586640)
- **Earlier revenue and losses (conflicting):** One report gives 2024 revenue of ₩63.1B (+9.5%) and says the operating loss widened from ₩18.8B (2020) to ₩85.2B (2023) before narrowing to ₩45.6B — [MTN, Aug 2026](https://news.mtn.co.kr/news-detail/2026082016094695664). This conflicts with Newsis: a 14.4% decline to ₩66.3B in 2025 implies about ₩77B in 2024, not ₩63.1B. Treat the 2023–2024 figures as uncertain and check them against DART.
- **Labour dispute:** MTN (Aug 2026) reports six years of capital impairment and a labour–management standoff over a wage freeze — [MTN](https://news.mtn.co.kr/news-detail/2026082016094695664)
- **Third-party estimate:** Preqin lists Naver Z revenue of about $46.3M and EBITDA of about −$42.5M for an unspecified recent year, and an Aug 2023 $13M round led by Jump Crypto — [Preqin](https://www.preqin.com/data/profile/asset/zepeto/394765)

### Inferences
- Avatar fashion is a proven spending trigger for young women in Asia: billions of items sold, and creators earning six figures. Even so, a cosmetics-only economy at about 20M MAU has not covered the costs of a 3D metaverse. Lifey's 2D layered-webp approach is far cheaper and better suited to a small team.
- Registered users (400M) vs MAU (20M) is roughly a 5% active ratio. That points to heavy novelty churn even at the category leader.
- Zepeto is pushing into live streaming and AI streamers to fix retention. This suggests that avatars plus outfits alone do not create a daily habit; there has to be a recurring reason to open the app.

### Gaps
- No recent (2024–2026) Zepeto MAU or DAU figure was found, and no Taiwan-specific user share. The "about 65% Asia" figure from secondary sources could not be verified.
- Currency details (ZEM/coins pricing, take rate on creator items) were not confirmed from primary sources in this pass.

## Locket Widget: growth mechanics, monetization (Locket Gold), and retention claims

### Takeaway
Locket began as a long-distance couple's gift and grew through a home-screen widget that shows close friends' photos live. Each friend graph is small (20 friends free; about 14 on average for teens). The company claims more than 80–90M downloads, about 9M DAU (Aug–Oct 2025), a 51% DAU/MAU ratio, and profitability since 2024 with about 10–14 staff. Locket Gold ($3.99/mo, launched 2024) sells video, unlimited friends and "who viewed". Its biggest non-US markets are Vietnam and Saudi Arabia, which makes it the most directly relevant model for Lifey's partner/family sharing.

### Cited Findings
- **Origin and early growth:** Matt Moss built Locket to send photos to his girlfriend during a long-distance relationship. It launched in Dec 2021 and had about 20M installs by Jun 2022 (Sensor Tower). At that point it ranked #9 in Social and #42 overall on the US App Store, and its popularity was already declining as competitors appeared. In this widget wave, push notifications and widgets replace feed browsing — [TechCrunch, 16 Jun 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/06/16/the-next-big-social-platform-isthe-smartphones-homescreen)
- **2025 metrics:** Over 80M total downloads and more than 9M DAU. Over 10B photos shared. Profitable since 2024. The widget updates with friends' latest photos, and each user can pick up to 20 close friends. "Celebrity Lockets" let artists open 1,000–15,000 fan slots; 17% of Suki Waterhouse's 5,000 fans were new to Locket — [TechCrunch, 6 Aug 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/06/photo-sharing-app-locket-is-banking-on-a-new-celebrity-focused-feature-to-fuel-its-growth)
- **Later scale:** Over 90M downloads and 9M DAU by Oct 2025. Profitable with a team of 14 — [VnExpress](https://e.vnexpress.net/news/tech/personalities/how-a-gift-for-his-girlfriend-changes-locket-founder-matt-moss-s-life-5106478.html)
- **2026 company claims:**
  - DAU up 22% since 1 Jan 2026; a 51% DAU/MAU ratio.
  - 47.6% of new US Gen Alpha users still active after one month.
  - Daily image sends up 38%, and images per user per week up 18%.
  - Teens have 14 friends on average.
  - No public feeds, algorithms or infinite scroll; AI moderation for bullying and self-harm.
  - Brand and creator campaigns, such as Warner Bros.' Wuthering Heights with 65K+ fans, and "Locket Looks" AI selfies with Google DeepMind.

  Source: [Locket press release via Webull, 15 Jul 2026](https://www.webull.com/news/15232982127436800). These figures are unaudited company claims.
- **Locket Gold and markets (Apr 2024):**
  - Gold costs $3.99/mo. Perks: videos up to 10s, uploads from the camera roll, longer captions, seeing who viewed your photos, and unlimited friends. Video was the most-wanted paid perk.
  - The free friend cap rose from 5 to 20 in Jun 2022.
  - Estimated revenue was about $100K/month (called "very low") and DAU about 3M, versus about 20M for BeReal.
  - The main DAU markets were Vietnam, Saudi Arabia and the US. Locket deliberately targeted SE Asia and the Middle East, where BeReal was weak.
  - Locket prioritised iOS, where users are more willing to pay.
  - Features were added steadily: likes, captions, emoji replies, DMs (Dec 2022), and location, weather and time stamps (Apr 2023).
  - The team was about 10 people.

  Source: [白鯨出海 Baijing, 30 Apr 2024](https://www.baijing.cn/article/48483)
- **Paywall growth:** Locket launched Gold in 2024. Helium, a paywall-tool vendor, claims Locket grew subscriptions 77% using its paywall testing — [Helium case study (vendor marketing)](https://tryhelium.com/blog/how-locket-grew-subscriptions-with-helium)

### Inferences
- The "someone I care about is here" pull comes from passive presence on the home screen. A partner's photo appears without opening the app, and each new photo invites a reply in kind. A widget of the partner's or family's latest entry, or their chibi in the room, is the closest analogue for Lifey. iOS PWAs cannot ship widgets, which is a structural limit for Lifey's PWA architecture; push notifications are the fallback.
- The couple use case is the original and most durable one. Locket's tiny graph (14–20 friends) still produces a 51% DAU/MAU ratio, much like Bondee's 50 cap. The difference is that Locket's daily *content* is effortless (one photo), while Bondee had no daily content loop.
- Monetization that works for close-friend apps is a subscription for richer expression (video, more friends, view receipts), not ads. Payers are concentrated on iOS. Revenue was modest in 2024 (about $1.2M/yr run-rate), but a very small team made it profitable.
- Vietnam's strength shows the format travels well in Asia. No Taiwan data was found.

- No independent figures for Locket Gold subscribers or revenue after 2024.
- No breakdown of couple vs. friend-group usage.

## Mechanics that create a daily "someone I care about is here" pull, and the causes of churn

### Takeaway
Daily pulls come from a partner or friends' fresh content landing passively in front of you (a widget or notification), with low effort to reply. Churn comes from novelty fade when there is no daily content loop (Bondee, BeReal), from empty or small friend graphs at the start, and from privacy or trust breaches, which were fatal in Taiwan.

### Cited Findings
- **Pull 1, passive presence:** A home-screen widget plus push notifications instead of feed browsing (Locket, BeReal WidgetMojis). TikTok hashtags drove awareness: #bereal had over 390M views and #liveinapp 279.5M. The photo archive was framed as the way to keep users from leaving — [TechCrunch, Jun 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/06/16/the-next-big-social-platform-isthe-smartphones-homescreen)
- **Pull 2, a small trusted circle with no feed, algorithm or infinite scroll:** This gives a 51% DAU/MAU ratio and 14 friends per teen on average — [Locket PR, Jul 2026](https://www.webull.com/news/15232982127436800)
- **Pull 3, the avatar as a mood/status display:** Room visits and notes (Bondee) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bondee); [Mindshare](https://www.mindshareworld.com/news/pov-what-is-bondee/)
- **Churn: boredom and an underpowered graph:** "Some users said they simply got bored"; the 50-member cap meant "too few people to generate traction" — [Straits Times / Bloomberg](https://cis.smu.edu.sg/sites/cis.smu.edu.sg/files/news/28%20Apr%202023%20-%20The%20Straits%20Times%20Online.pdf)
- **Churn: trust:** "If people feel misled or unsafe, they aren't going to stick around" — [same](https://cis.smu.edu.sg/sites/cis.smu.edu.sg/files/news/28%20Apr%202023%20-%20The%20Straits%20Times%20Online.pdf)
- **BeReal contrast, fading after a once-a-day mechanic:**
  - DAUs fell 61% between Oct 2022 and Mar 2023 (Sensor Tower via NYT), from about 15M to under 6M. BeReal itself claimed 20M DAU in Apr 2023 — [Engadget](https://engadget.com/bereal-the-buzziest-app-of-2022-has-been-bought-by-a-mobile-game-publisher-175016152.html). Platformer framed the same drop as MAU, so the metric definition is disputed.
  - Voodoo acquired BeReal for €500M in Jun 2024 and cited 40M "active users" — [Engadget](https://engadget.com/bereal-the-buzziest-app-of-2022-has-been-bought-by-a-mobile-game-publisher-175016152.html); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/bereal-sold-to-french-video-game-developer-for-500-million/718675/); [Fortune](https://www.fortune.com/2024/06/14/the-riddle-of-the-bereal-deal)
  - Users had mixed reactions to celebrity features ("RealPeople") because the app was valued as an escape from celebrities — [TechCrunch, Aug 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/06/photo-sharing-app-locket-is-banking-on-a-new-celebrity-focused-feature-to-fuel-its-growth)
- **Zepeto's response to weak habit:** Pushing live streaming and AI streamers to lengthen retention — [Newsis, Apr 2026](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20260410_0003586640)

### Inferences
- **For Lifey:**
  1. Make the solo daily loop (entry → room or doll reacts → mood garden grows) satisfying on its own, so the app survives an empty graph.
  2. Make the partner's or family's activity visible in the room, for example their chibi visiting or a letter on the desk, and send a gentle notification when they post. This creates the "someone is here" pull without a public feed.
  3. Keep the circle small and private (partner and family). That matches the Bondee/Locket appeal, and Lifey avoids Bondee's growth problem because it doesn't need virality to be useful.
  4. Lead with transparent data handling as a Taiwan-market differentiator after Bondee.
  5. Monetize later with a subscription (more outfits or rooms, video memories, more family members) rather than a coin economy or anything crypto.
- A Lunar New Year or holiday launch with invite links worked for Bondee's acquisition spike. Without a daily loop, though, a spike is wasted.

### Gaps
- No public research isolates the causal weight of each churn factor; this is drawn from press narratives.
- No Taiwan-specific retention data exists for any of these apps.
