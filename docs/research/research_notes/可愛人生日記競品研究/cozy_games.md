# Cozy / Life-Sim Games: Daily Return Loops, Decoration Economies, Monetization (input for Lifey)

Scope: Animal Crossing (Pocket Camp, New Horizons), Neko Atsume 1/2, The Sims, 大頭妹 (Korean flash character games), Cyworld minihompy. Research date: 2026-10-20. All figures carry the date of the source data. Several key sources (Game Developer interviews, Nintendo IR PDFs, Dcard, TweakTown) returned 403 or 404 or were not reachable, and those gaps are listed below.

## 1. Which daily-return mechanics are most cited?

### Takeaway
The return hooks that hold up are low-effort, real-time "something happened while you were away" surprises: Neko Atsume cats and fish gifts, Pocket Camp's rotating visitor requests and friendship levels, and Cyworld's social visits, guestbook and ilchon update notifications. Hard timers sold for currency are the most criticised.

### Cited Findings
**Neko Atsume (Hit-Point; 2014-10-20 JP/global; English version 2015-10-30)**
- Core loop: put out food and goods, cats visit and play, and you photograph them for an album. Cats sometimes leave gifts of fish and collectible "mementos". After a cat leaves it may leave silver or gold *niboshi* (fish), which buy new food, goods, yard remodels and extensions. — [Wikipedia: Neko Atsume](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neko_Atsume)
- Daily password: entering it gives a random amount of silver or gold fish, and five passwords earn a free can of food. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neko_Atsume)
- Collection depth: 86 cats as of v1.15.0, 26 of them rare. Rare cats appear only when specific items or foods are placed, and there is no end state. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neko_Atsume)
- Design intent, from creator Yutaka Takazaki: players "do not have to take too much time or energy… nor be a hardcore gamer." The game was conceived as a "look and enjoy" experience and was inspired by cat cafés. — quoted via [Kill Screen](https://www.killscreen.com/cats-finally-take-over-the-world-with-mobile-game-neko-atsume/) / [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neko_Atsume)
- Hidden rules (which item attracts which rare cat) are never explained, so fans worked them out and shared tips. That became word-of-mouth. — [Kill Screen](https://www.killscreen.com/cats-finally-take-over-the-world-with-mobile-game-neko-atsume/)
- The game won the CEDEC Game Design award in August 2015. — [Kill Screen](https://www.killscreen.com/cats-finally-take-over-the-world-with-mobile-game-neko-atsume/)

**Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp (Nintendo/DeNA; 2017-11-21 global)**
- Loop: gather materials and fulfil animal visitors' requests, earning materials and friendship XP. Placing an animal's favourite furniture attracts them to your campsite. Relationships grow through daily tasks and chats, and the alpaca Cyrus crafts furniture. — [Wikipedia: Pocket Camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Crossing:_Pocket_Camp)
- Friendship levels unlock rewards at each level. Shovelstrike Quarry is free if 5 other players help, which works as a social hook. — [PocketGamer.biz, 2017-10-27](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/how-does-animal-crossing-pocket-camp-monetise/)
- Fruit regrows in about 3 hours (24 hours in console Animal Crossing), so mobile sessions are shorter and more frequent. — [PocketGamer.biz, 2017-10-27](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/how-does-animal-crossing-pocket-camp-monetise/)
- Five-star Fortune Cookie items unlock a "Scrapbook Memory" for the related villager, tying collection to relationships. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Crossing:_Pocket_Camp)
- The "Merry Memories Plan" (2022-01-27) added planner customisation and step-tracking integration. This is a journaling-adjacent feature inside a cozy game. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Crossing:_Pocket_Camp)

**Cyworld (Korea, minihompy from 2002-09)**
- Ilchon (1촌, "first-degree friend") requests unlock restricted diary and photo sections. "Interest ilchons" get update notifications and online status, so the reason to come back is social: checking who visited and who updated. — [Wikipedia: Cyworld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyworld)
- Minihompy includes a background, profile, **diary**, photo album, guestbook and BGM. The Miniroom is an **isometric room with an avatar ("minime")**, decorated with items bought with dotori. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyworld)
- Dotori were commonly given to ilchons as **birthday gifts**. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyworld)

**大頭妹 (Taiwan name for a Korean flash character)**
- Taiwanese/Chinese wikis describe 大頭妹 as "a virtual character in Korean games", called "Panypang" there. The series includes 《大頭妹的美妙人生》1 & 2 (walk around her home and school, click objects to trigger life events), 《大頭妹的房間》, 《大頭妹可愛裝扮》 (dress-up, which only clears when the outfit matches), a hair-salon game and a cake game. — [Newton 百科: 大頭妹](https://www.newton.com.tw/wiki/%E5%A4%A7%E9%A0%AD%E5%A6%B9); [小遊戲谷 tag](https://gamekuo.com/tags/big-head-sister); [4399 大頭妹專題](https://www.4399.com/special/top-25.htm)
- These are remembered as childhood nostalgia ("以前玩大頭妹…可以玩一整天"), and Flash games have been unplayable in browsers since 2021. — [Newton/gamekuo search results](https://gamekuo.com/content/913); [Dcard girl forum thread (403, title only)](https://www.dcard.tw/f/girl/p/240780663)

### Inferences
- For a journal app, the closest analogue to Neko Atsume's "visitors while away" is: write an entry, and by the next open a visitor (pet, friend, NPC) has arrived or left a small gift or letter in the room. This rewards coming back without punishing absence.
- A daily password or daily gift (Neko Atsume) is a low-cost, non-punishing daily hook. A "5 days → bonus" counter is softer than a streak that resets.
- Hidden, discoverable rules (rare visitors triggered by specific furniture) drive sharing in a 13–35 female audience on Threads, IG and Dcard.
- Cyworld shows that **diary + isometric room + avatar + BGM + friend visits** was already a proven combination at national scale. Lifey's room plus diary is a direct descendant, and the social layer (家人互看, guestbook) is the strongest return driver.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm the original Korean title or developer of 大頭妹/"Panypang". Searches for Panypang, 패니팡 and "Dalkomm" returned nothing. The Dcard thread on childhood Korean web games returned 403. It may be a Korean character-site flash series (c. 2000s), but this is unverified.
- No developer-sourced explanation for why Neko Atsume gifts are given on departure. A Japanese CEDEC talk would be the next source.
- No Pocket Camp data on DAU or retention.

## 2. Pocket Camp revenue, shutdown, and monetization lessons (Fortune Cookies, Pocket Camp Club, Complete)

### Takeaway
Pocket Camp earned roughly $250M+ over 7 years, about two-thirds of it from Japan. It used a dual currency (Bells soft, Leaf Tickets hard), timer skips, gacha "Fortune Cookies" and subscriptions. The gacha drew the strongest backlash, including the loss of the Belgian market. Revenue fell to about $1.5M a month by 2023–24, and Nintendo turned it into a $19.99 offline paid "Complete" edition.

### Cited Findings
**Revenue and players (dated)**
- $50M by 2018-09, over $150M by 2020-04, and over $250M lifetime player spending by 2021-11 (Sensor Tower). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Crossing:_Pocket_Camp); [Sensor Tower blog](https://sensortower.com/blog/animal-crossing-pocket-camp-250-million-revenue)
- As of 2021-11: $255.7M lifetime, Nintendo's #2 mobile game by spending. Japan contributed $172M (67.3%). — [Sensor Tower via search summary](https://sensortower.com/blog/animal-crossing-pocket-camp-250-million-revenue); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/animal-crossing-pocket-camp-surpasses-250-million)
- Best month was 2020-04 at about $7.9M, driven by the New Horizons launch (2020-03). Downloads rose from 4.5M in 2020-03 to about 7.1M in 2020-04. — [Sensor Tower 150M blog](https://sensortower.com/blog/animal-crossing-pocket-camp-revenue-150-million); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Crossing:_Pocket_Camp)
- AppMagic estimate (2024-08): over $260M lifetime from about 64M downloads, with a steady $1.4–1.7M per month since 2023-04. — [mobilegamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/animal-crossing-pocket-camp-to-close-as-nintendos-slow-retreat-from-mobile-continues/)

**Monetization design**
- Leaf Tickets cost A$1.49 for 20 up to A$62.99 for 1,200 (2017-10 soft launch). They buy fertiliser (5, instant fruit), a second crafting slot (80, about A$5), quarry entry (20), and special nets and honey (15). A 72-hour starter pack offered 40 tickets plus items for A$1.49. — [PocketGamer.biz, 2017-10-27](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/how-does-animal-crossing-pocket-camp-monetise/)
- Some items are unlockable only with Leaf Tickets. Leaf Tickets also substitute for materials and buy special event furniture. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Crossing:_Pocket_Camp)
- Fortune Cookies cost 50 Leaf Tickets or 5,000 Bells. Each draws from 10 limited 3–5★ items, and stamp cards let 10 stamps buy a chosen item ("pity"). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Crossing:_Pocket_Camp)
- Pocket Camp Club launched 2019-11-21: Happy Helper Plan $2.99/month (+60 tickets per renewal) and Furniture & Fashion Plan $7.99/month (5 Fortune Cookies per month). Merry Memories Plan launched 2022-01-27. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Crossing:_Pocket_Camp)

**Backlash**
- Fortune Cookie loot boxes led Nintendo to cut off Belgian users from 2019-08-27. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Crossing:_Pocket_Camp)
- Destructoid: the best loot is tied to fortune cookies, and changes "focused on pushing players to spend money on Leaf Tickets." One reviewer dropped the score to 0.5/5 when cookies were added. — [Destructoid](https://destructoid.com/?p=234488); [HonestGamers](https://www.honestgamers.com/13650/android/animal-crossing-pocket-camp/review.html)
- Launch criticism (2017) said the game "lacks the heart and soul." Tech Advisor wrote that heavy play hits "frustrating walls of timers and micro-transactions," while occasional play "leaves a lot to love." — [Christian Post roundup](https://www.christianpost.com/trends/animal-crossing-pocket-camp-review-roundup.html); [Tech Advisor](https://www.techadvisor.com/article/718513/animal-crossing-pocket-camp-review-2.html)
- Counterpoint: PocketGamer.biz (2017-10) said the timers were "kept to an absolute minimum." Metacritic score 72/100. — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/how-does-animal-crossing-pocket-camp-monetise/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Crossing:_Pocket_Camp)

**Shutdown and Complete**
- Announced 2024-08-21. The free service ended 2024-11-28. Nintendo gave no detailed reason. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Crossing:_Pocket_Camp); [Animal Crossing World](https://animalcrossingworld.com/2024/08/animal-crossing-pocket-camp-live-service-shuts-down-this-fall-offline-paid-version-coming-soon/)
- Context: President Furukawa said in 2024-05 that Nintendo is not "looking to continue releasing many new applications for the mobile market." He framed mobile as broadening the fan base, with Pocket Camp as a feeder for New Horizons. Dr. Mario World and Dragalia Lost had already closed. — [mobilegamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/animal-crossing-pocket-camp-to-close-as-nintendos-slow-retreat-from-mobile-continues/); [MCV/UK](https://www.mcvuk.com/business-news/nintendos-mobile-titles-have-an-impact-beyond-direct-mobile-revenue-says-furukawa/). Revenue decline as the cause is press inference, not Nintendo's stated reason.
- Pocket Camp Complete was released 2024-12-02. It cost $9.99 until 2025-01-31, then $19.99. It is a one-time purchase with no IAP, Leaf Tickets, Club or multiplayer, and it plays offline. It includes 7 years of content (minus some collaboration items such as Sanrio), earnable "Leaf Tokens", ex-Club features, and save transfer of items and layouts (Leaf Tickets not transferred). — [Animal Crossing World](https://animalcrossingworld.com/2024/10/animal-crossing-pocket-camp-complete-removes-microtransactions-costs-10-launching-in-december/); [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2024/10/animal-crossing-pocket-camp-complete-arrives-on-mobile-this-december); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Crossing:_Pocket_Camp)
- Complete made about $2.6M in its first week and over $3M by 2024-12-09, with a $505K day on 2024-12-08. That first week roughly equals two months of late-era F2P revenue. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Crossing:_Pocket_Camp) (cites app-intelligence data)

**Animal Crossing: New Horizons (context)**
- Sold 13.41M units in its first 6 weeks (2020). It sold 5M digital copies in one month, a record at the time, and reached 50.29M units per Nintendo's 2026-08-06 earnings. — [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/150276/animal-crossing-new-horizons-50-million-units-sold); [iMore](https://www.imore.com/animal-crossing-new-horizons-sold-over-11-million)

### Inferences
- Lessons for Lifey:
  - (a) Keep the earn loop (diary → coins) free and generous.
  - (b) Never gate cute furniture behind gacha. Loot boxes caused legal exits (Belgium) and the harshest reviews.
  - (c) Timer-skip monetization fits poorly with a journaling cadence of 1 entry per day.
  - (d) A small subscription or cosmetic-pack model is less toxic. The Club's $2.99 tier shows that low-priced, convenience-oriented subs were accepted.
  - (e) Players valued keeping their layouts and items when the service changed. The Complete save transfer shows decorated rooms are emotional assets, so Lifey's backup and sync of the room matters.
- Japan made up 67% of revenue, a sign that East Asian female-skewing audiences spend on cute decoration. This is relevant for Taiwan, but there is no Taiwan-specific number.
- A collection organised by season or theme, with a "pity" stamp card, is the fairer version of limited items. Lifey could sell seasonal sets directly for coins, with no randomness.

### Gaps
- No public Pocket Camp DAU, retention, or Taiwan revenue split. No Nintendo IR breakdown per title (Nintendo reports only aggregate "mobile, IP-related income").
- The TweakTown article on Nintendo mobile earnings returned 403.
- The Fortune Cookie price is flagged in Wikipedia as coming from a questionable source.

## 3. Cyworld economy (dotori, minihompy), cultural impact, relaunches

### Takeaway
Cyworld proved that people will pay small amounts for self-expression (room furniture, skins, BGM) attached to a personal diary and homepage. About 70–80% of revenue came from dotori-bought virtual goods at its 2006 peak. It declined on mobile, Facebook and Kakao competition, and a 2011 data breach. Relaunches in 2021–2022 drew nostalgia spikes, but lost data, empty friend lists and thin content made them fizzle.

### Cited Findings
- Dotori ("acorn") cost 1 = ₩100 and was coined in 2002. It bought wall art (about 2 dotori), miniroom songs (about 6), and a year-long background (about 40, citation needed). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyworld)
- In 2006, a skin cost about $1 per week and BGM about £0.30. Digital items were 78% of revenue, mobile 10% and ads 12%. — [The Register, 2006-06-23](https://www.theregister.com/2006/06/23/cyworld_comes_to_germany/)
- CNN (2006-07) reported projected 2006 sales of about $140M, with acorns about 70%. — [CNN Money](https://money.cnn.com/2006/07/27/technology/cyworld0727.biz2/index.htm)
- In 2009, the press reported over ₩112.2B (about $98.7M) from dotori in 2009, while SK Comms estimated ₩70–80B. Sources conflict, and the reported drop was tied to users moving to Facebook and Twitter. — [Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/cyworld-looks-to-nate-for-ideas/10424787)
- Users: 19M domestic in 2006, over 20M in early 2007 (about half of South Korea), and "nearly every South Korean in their twenties" by 2005. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyworld)
- Decline: Facebook entered Korea in 2009, KakaoStory grew on the back of KakaoTalk, and a July 2011 breach exposed 35M+ users. The overseas versions closed: Germany/Europe by 2008, Japan in 2008-08 (lost to Mixi), and the US in 2010-02. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyworld)
- The service shut down in 2019-10. At closure, about 2.76M members still held dotori, with a total balance of about ₩3.84B, which became a consumer-protection issue. — [search summary of Korean press: segye/inews24](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20220402506497)
- 2021 preview (Cyworld Z): over 4M visitors in a day. "Cyworld BGM 2021" had artists remake classic BGM hits. — [Korea JoongAng Daily 2021](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/dust-off-that-old-account-cyworld-is-making-a-comeback/10671714); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyworld)
- The app relaunched 2022-04-02 and hit #1 free on Google Play and the App Store. However, the photo album and diary were only partly restored, reviews complained that guestbooks and diaries had been erased, and dormant ilchons made the experience feel empty ("친구들 어딨니"). Press called it "속 빈 강정" (all hype, no content). — [Newsis 2022-04-02](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20220402_0001818242); [byline.network](https://byline.network/2022/04/4-132/); [Tech42](https://www.tech42.co.kr/이거뿐이야-재오픈한-싸이월드-즐길-콘텐츠-없는/)
- The service was suspended for "renewal" in 2023-08. In 2024-11, Cycom (Cycomz) acquired the assets, including about 17 billion user photos, and plans a new community service. — [Kyunghyang 2024-11-13](https://www.khan.co.kr/article/202411131639147/amp); [Daum 2024-11-13](https://v.daum.net/v/20241113163931418)

### Inferences
- The Cyworld economy maps directly onto Lifey: a diary page and an isometric mini-room, with a soft currency for furniture, BGM and skins. One difference is that Lifey's coins would be earned by writing, not bought, which removes the "pay to express" barrier.
- Dotori as a gift between friends is a strong social mechanic. Lifey could let family or partners gift coins or furniture.
- Cyworld's failed relaunch shows that **users' memories (diary and photos) are the real asset**. Losing them destroys trust, which reinforces Lifey's sync and backup priorities.
- Rooms only matter socially if friends visit, and an empty network kills the return loop.

### Gaps
- There is no reliable figure for 2022 relaunch downloads or MAU, and no confirmation that a 2025–2026 relaunch happened.
- There is no breakdown of dotori revenue by item type (BGM vs. furniture vs. skins).

## 4. Sims-style needs, moods, aspirations: what helps vs. creates pressure

### Takeaway
The Sims chain runs from needs (decaying meters) to moodlets to emotion, with aspirations and whims rewarding goal completion. Players praise the expressive emotions but complain about fast-decaying needs and volatile moods, and often switch decay off. For a journal app, mood should be *reported* by the user and *reflected* in the room, never a meter that drains.

### Cited Findings
- In The Sims 4 (2014), needs feed emotions. Neglected needs create negative moodlets (Hungry, Lonely) that push the Sim toward Sad, Angry or Tense. — [Prima Games eGuide](https://primagames.com/eguides/the-sims-4-eguide/feelings-lots-of-feelings); [SimsCommunity preview 2014-08-15](https://simscommunity.info/2014/08/15/the-sims-4-preview-emotions-needs/)
- Criticism: emotions are "completely ridiculous and out of proportion." A Sim can go to bed elated and wake up in a rage. — [Her Campus review](https://www.hercampus.com/school/uct/sims-4-honest-review/). On the EA forums, players say grief is overridden too easily and emotions "change too quickly." — [EA Forums](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/the-sims-4-feedback-en/issue-with-the-emotion-system/13533725) (user opinion)
- Players commonly turn off motive decay. Aspirations and whims earn Satisfaction Points, which buy rewards such as the "Moodlet Solver" (500 pts, clears negative moodlets). Aspirations therefore double as a mood-management resource. — [cyberpost guide](https://cyberpost.co/?p=100737)

### Inferences
- **Helps:**
  - Expressive mood shown on the avatar. Lifey already maps diary emotion to face via `faceFor`.
  - Optional aspirations and goals with rewards, such as "write about 3 places this month".
  - Life stages, as a framing for a lifelong journal (birth year, ages).
- **Creates pressure:**
  - Decaying needs meters, where the character becomes "sad" if you skip a day.
  - Streak-reset penalties.
  - Moods that swing for trivial reasons.
- For a 13–35 female audience journaling real feelings, the room reflecting a sad entry (rain on the window, a cosy blanket) is empathetic. A punishment for absence would be guilt-inducing.

### Gaps
- I found no Maxis developer interview on the intended design of needs and emotions. Life stages and plumbob mechanics were not researched in depth (the sources found covered only emotions, needs and aspirations).

## 5. Fixed slots vs. free placement; pricing and seasonal items (cross-cutting)

### Takeaway
The evidence is thin, but the pattern is consistent: soft currency is earned through the core activity (Bells, Neko fish), and premium or limited items drive spend (Leaf Ticket event furniture, Fortune Cookie seasonal sets, Cyworld dotori skins). Seasonal and limited content is the main spending trigger.

### Cited Findings
- Pocket Camp: Bells come from play and item sales. Leaf Tickets buy special event furniture, and Fortune Cookies carry 10 limited themed items each. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Crossing:_Pocket_Camp); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/how-does-animal-crossing-pocket-camp-monetise/)
- Neko Atsume: all goods are bought with fish earned from cats, and fish can also be bought with real money. Seven yard remodel styles exist. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neko_Atsume)
- Neko Atsume places goods in **fixed slots** in the yard (by design, items go into predefined positions), while the Pocket Camp campsite and Cyworld miniroom used free or grid placement. — [Wikipedia: Neko Atsume](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neko_Atsume) mentions yard and placement, but the fixed-slot detail is from general knowledge and should be verified.
- Downloads: Neko Atsume reached 4M by 2015-05 and 10M by 2015-12-04, with up to 40% from the West before any English version. The series has had 30M downloads (date unclear, per Meta blog). Neko Atsume 2 launched 2024-10. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neko_Atsume); [Meta blog](https://www.meta.com/blog/neko-atsume-interview/)

### Inferences
- Neko Atsume's fixed slots fit a casual phone room and suit Lifey's pre-drawn isometric art, where free placement would need a sprite for every rotation and position. Offering a few anchor slots per room (wall, floor-left, desk) would keep the art polished.
- Price anchoring: if 1 diary entry ≈ X coins, a common item should cost about 2–3 entries and a seasonal centrepiece about 2–3 weeks of writing. These numbers are speculative and would need tuning.

### Gaps
- I found no Sensor Tower data on Neko Atsume 2 downloads or revenue. No source covers Neko Atsume's IAP revenue.
- There is no hard data comparing fixed slots and free placement for retention or monetization.
